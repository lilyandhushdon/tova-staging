Featured concept

Maison Marée

Brand identity Website Bookings

The brief

An imagined three-room guesthouse on a Banks Peninsula bay, relying on third-party booking sites and losing a slice of every stay to commission.

What we made

A quiet, French-inflected identity and a fast website with direct booking, room galleries and a local guide that gives guests a reason to book here first.

What we'd measure

Share of direct bookings, enquiry-to-booking rate, and how often guests find the site through search rather than a booking platform.
